Projects & Activities

Data Analytics Club - Project Manager

BioKind, UW-Madison

03/2025 – Present

I first joined BioKind out of a general interest in data analysis, then gradually grew from a club member to Team Lead and later Project Manager.

  • In this role, I guide team members through different stages of data analysis, review their work, and help identify additional charts needed for final deliverables.
  • I strengthened my skills in R, ggplot2, GeoPandas, data visualization, accessibility-minded chart design, public speaking, and self-directed learning.
  • Most importantly, I learned to not be scared to ask questions or reach out, turning analysis into clear presentations for real community partners, including presenting our findings in person to leaders at the Boys & Girls Clubs of Dane County.

Healthy Meals Website - UX Designer Most Promising Solution Award

Badgers For Good Design-a-thon, UW-Madison

04/2026

This was my most memorable one-day, hackathon-style UX design competition where my team worked with Healthy School Meals for All to improve their website.

  • Although the original task only required redesigning one section, I proposed a more ambitious full-website redesign within 8 hours.
  • The coolest feature that we embedded in the website was an interactive map for users to find state senator contact info, along with 3 message templates so they can directly advocate their thoughts on school food health, far exceeding the original competition requirements.
  • I’ll always remember the importance of rapid prototyping and ability to deliver a polished presentation under pressure.

MadDining Website - Website Design

MadCoders Symposium, UW-Madison

09/2025 - Present

MadDining started from my own experience trying to compare UW–Madison dining options while ranking dorms and realizing there was no centralized place for student dining reviews.

  • At MadCoders, I led a team of three to turn this idea into a website where current students can share dining hall reviews and incoming Badgers can consider food options when choosing dorms.
  • Through this project, I practiced turning real student needs into a web product, while strengthening my skills in project planning, user-centered design, and front-end development.
